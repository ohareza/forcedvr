# Privacy policy

Force enable YouTube DVR does not collect or transmit personal information,
browsing history, cookies, video identifiers, or analytics.

The extension runs bundled code on supported YouTube pages to adjust playback
settings. It makes no network requests of its own and downloads no remote code.
YouTube continues to handle video playback and its own data collection.

The on/off preference is stored with the browser's sync storage. Your browser may
sync that setting across devices according to your browser account settings.

The toolbar action reloads the active YouTube page when you toggle the extension.
